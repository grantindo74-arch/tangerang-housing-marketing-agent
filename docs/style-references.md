# Style references

Two reference videos shared on 2026-10-19. Both are 10s, 720×1280, 24 fps. The look of both suggests they're AI-generated. Our versions will use **real Anggrek Permai footage** in the same styles, at 15s.

## Style A — "Promo Punch" (ref: *Promo Cicilan 4 Jutaan*)

Energetic and sales-focused.

| Time | Shot | Overlay |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Aerial/drone over rows of houses | Big 3D-style title "RUMAH SIAP HUNI" (red + yellow, white outline, house icon), punch-in with a lens flare |
| 2–4s | Living room, slow push-in | Title shrinks away |
| 4–7s | Kitchen, slow pan | Magenta/pink ribbon banner "CICILAN 4 JUTAAN / BULAN" slides in |
| 7–8.5s | Playground with kids | Banner stays |
| 8.5–10s | Main gate with "ANGGREK PERMAI" sign | End card: location-pin house icon + "ANGGREK PERMAI — TANGERANG" on a purple→red gradient bar |

- About 2s per shot, with fast cuts and loud, upbeat music.
- Bold sans-serif type, saturated brand colors (red, yellow, magenta, purple).

## Style B — "Cinematic Family" (ref: *Rumah Impian Keluarga*)

Warm, emotional and aspirational.

| Time | Shot | Overlay |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Family breakfast on a terrace, golden hour | Elegant white serif "RUMAH IMPIAN KELUARGA" fades in |
| 4–7s | Kids running in the garden | Title fades out |
| 7–10s | Family on the sofa in the evening, warm lamp light | Logo end card: roof icon + "ANGGREK PERMAI — TANGERANG" in white serif |

- 3 long shots with slow camera drift and soft fades, and gentle, quieter music.
- A warm color grade and minimal text.

## Plan for our 15s versions

- **Property shots** (exterior, interiors, gate, amenities, aerial) come from **real footage only**.
- **People / lifestyle shots:** use real footage, or AI-generated clips (labeled as AI) where they don't misrepresent the property.
- **Claims** in overlays, such as "Cicilan 4 jutaan/bulan" or "Siap huni", must come from `config/approved-facts`.
- Style A becomes the template for the Price and promos and Amenities themes. Style B becomes the template for Lifestyle and Home tour.
- 15s gives each style 1–2 extra shots. The end card stays at about 2s.
