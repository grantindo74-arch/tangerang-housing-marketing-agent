⚠️ needs care before reuse:
- **"All In 5 Juta Dapat Rumah 2 Lantai"** reads as if the house costs 5 juta. The approved fact is a 5 juta booking fee; confirm what "all in" covers and say "Booking 5 juta" instead.
- **"Unit Terbatas / Sold Out"** is a scarcity claim with no approved basis (the form gave no unit count).

The cover backgrounds are rendered (not real) images of the houses, and about 20 covers carry a visible "Canva" watermark from unlicensed Pro elements. Don't repost those covers as they are.

## Rules carried over

- Never use "termurah" (banned by the team).
- Prices, cicilan, DP and free-cost claims always appear with "*Syarat & ketentuan berlaku" until the team decides otherwise.
- Don't promise investment returns; frame ownership as building an asset, not a guaranteed gain.
