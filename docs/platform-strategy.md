# Platform strategy

Agreed with the team on 2026-10-21. Every video is planned for one platform first; the same footage can be cut twice, but the two edits should feel different.

| | **TikTok** | **Instagram (Reels)** |
|---|---|---|
| Feel | Real, raw, fun | Polished, beautiful, calm. A sense of tranquility and happiness |
| Footage | Real phone footage, handheld is fine | The best, steadiest shots: slides, reveals, lights-on, golden light, details |
| Length | Short: ~15 s | Longer: ~30–45 s (to confirm) |
| Pace | Fast cuts, ~2 s per shot | Slow: long shots of 3–5 s, crossfades, gentle movement |
| Sound | Trending sound from TikTok's Commercial Music Library, added in the app | Calm, warm music (acoustic, soft piano, lo-fi) from Instagram's library or royalty-free |
| Language | Casual Gen Z Bahasa: "bisa dong", "yuk", "spill", "POV", "no galau" | More formal, warm Bahasa: "Hunian nyaman untuk keluarga Anda" |
| On-screen text | White on black or black on white, middle of screen, emoji, 1–2 short facts | Clean, minimal, elegant: fewer words, softer type, little or no emoji |
| Colour | Natural, as shot | Light, warm grade; brighter and softer |
| End card | Logo, one offer, WhatsApp number (green on white) | Logo, one offer, WhatsApp number; calmer layout |
| Post caption | One or two short lines, WhatsApp, a few hashtags | Can be longer and more descriptive, still easy to skim |

**What they have in common**

- **Real footage only**, all from the show unit, with no stock or people.
- **Logo** navy, top-centre, large (about 45% of frame width), on every shot.
- **Text** in the middle of the frame, Poppins-style bold. Mostly **white with a dark outline/shadow and no box**; VT 29 uses **black on white boxes**. Emoji used as markers (✅, 📍).
- **Text builds up:** a second line is added on the next shot ("Rumah Siap Huni" → + "FREE DP 0%"), so each screen has text and facts arrive one at a time.
- **CTA over real footage, not a separate end card:** a line like "Yuk, lihat unitnya langsung" plus a **white pill with a green phone icon and the number in green**, often with **📍 Tangerang, Banten** under it. It stays on screen for the last 3–5 s.
- **Pace:** a new shot about every 2–3 s; quick flash, whip or light-leak transitions between some shots.
- **Music** is mixed into the file.

**Watch-outs seen in the references:** VT 4 runs "UNIT TERBATAS!" (no approved unit count) over the stained back-yard wall (IMG_1410–1412, flagged "avoid" in the catalog). VT 18 says "FREE DP 0%"; the approved wording is "DP 0%".

## What stays the same on both

- **Every screen has text.** Each shot either keeps the previous shot's text or shows a new short text; no shot is ever left without text.
- Facts only from `config/approved-facts.json`, with the disclaimer next to price, DP and cicilan claims.
- The logo block top-centre (see `docs/brand-style.md`).
- Real footage for anything showing the property.
